# Workflow: Monetization Audit

Score each 1–5 and fix anything ≤ 3:
1. **Catalog breadth**: cosmetics, convenience, social/troll, currency packs, starter
   pack, VIP/subscription, private servers.
2. **Visibility**: are paid cosmetics seen by others in normal play? Preview pads?
3. **Desire**: do items look/sound great? Are they shown in action (previews, other
   players, leaderboard titles)?
4. **Price ladder**: impulse (≤ 49), mid (99–299), premium (399–999+). Bonus % on packs.
5. **Placement & timing**: contextual prompts, shop always reachable, no early spam.
6. **Fairness & policy**: completable free; paid randomness and PvP advantages only if the creator chose them (logged in DECISIONS.md) and done compliantly.
7. **Technical**: owned-on-join and mid-session grants, idempotent receipts, "Coming
   soon" for ID 0, telemetry on prompts and purchases.
8. **Data** (post-launch): conversion, top sellers, funnel drop-offs → rebalance.
Update `MONETIZATION.md` with changes and reasoning.
