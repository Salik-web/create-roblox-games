# Workflow: Polish Pass

Play as (a) a brand-new player on a phone, (b) a returning player, (c) a payer.
Capture screenshots throughout. Then improve, in this priority order:

1. **First 60 seconds**: goal clear in 10 s? Fun/funny/scary moment early? Reward
   within a minute? Fix confusion first — it's the biggest retention lever.
2. **Readability**: paths, objectives and interactables stand out (light, colour,
   highlights, beams).
3. **Visual quality** (`world-art.md`): lighting moods, atmosphere, set dressing,
   empty areas, material variety, consistent style, sky, hero props via generation.
4. **Game feel** (`animation-vfx-feel.md`): feedback stacks on every action and reward.
5. **Audio** (`audio.md`): ambience per area, SFX on everything, music transitions, mix.
6. **UI** (`ui-ux.md`): consistency, animation, phone layout, input prompts per device.
7. **Monetization presentation**: shop appeal, previews, visible cosmetics in world,
   prompt timing (`monetization-audit.md`).
8. **Performance** quick check (`performance.md`).
Record changes in PROGRESS.md, `save_place`, reply with a short before/after list.
