# Business: DevEx, Taxes, Ownership

Verify current numbers with docs before quoting them; they change.

## DevEx (Robux → USD)
- Standard rate ≈ $0.0038 per earned Robux (since Sep 2025); a higher rate (≈ $0.0054)
  applies to qualifying purchases by age-verified US 18+ players in eligible games
  (R15 avatars only, compliance checks).
- Minimum cash-out 30,000 earned Robux. Requirements include: 13+, verified email,
  ID verification, account in good standing, accepted DevEx terms, tax info.
- Payouts via Tipalti (bank wire, etc.). Transaction fees + 1.9–3% FX fee if converted.
- Submit a valid tax form (non-US: W-8BEN with country and tax ID; claim treaty rate if
  your country has one with the US). Without valid tax info, 24% US backup
  withholding may apply.
- Earned Robux may be pending for a few days after sales.

## Revenue math
Price × 0.7 (marketplace fee) × DevEx rate = USD per sale. Example: 99 R$ pass →
69 earned R$ → ≈ $0.26. Keep a `REVENUE.md` per month at project level.

## Ownership
- Personal account first (free). Move games to a Roblox Group later (group creation
  costs Robux) for revenue splits with collaborators and cleaner branding.
- Collaborators: Team Create (age-checked), percentage splits via group payouts.
  Write agreements down (even informal) before sharing revenue.

## Account security
2-Step Verification, unique strong password, never share cookies/session, review
Studio plugins (malicious plugins can steal or backdoor games), keep a backup of every
place file (File → Save to File) in the project folder.

## Taxes (local)
DevEx income is foreign income in most countries; keep payout records and ask a local
tax adviser how to declare it. (Claude is not a tax adviser.)

## Other income from the same skills
Scripting commissions, selling systems/models/plugins on the Creator Store, UGC
accessories, building for other studios.
